      Dichotomy Matrix: Transformative Dividends vs Existential Constitutional Perils

      ✓ Operational & Access Dividends
      • Turnaround Acceleration: Reduces registry scrutiny latency from 3 weeks to under 30 seconds.
      • Linguistic Inclusivity: 16+ Indian regional languages translated via SUVAS (Article 39A).
      • De-cluttering Rosters: Automated settlement of non-contested traffic e-Challans.
      • Undertrial Rights: Cryptographic instant delivery of bail via FASTER (Article 21).
      ⚠ Constitutional & Ethical Perils
      • Black-Box Inscrutability: Inability to generate reasoned speaking orders violates natural justice.
      • Re-encoding Societal Biases: Predictive scoring risks institutionalizing prejudice against marginalized groups.
      • Digital Stratification: Rural trial courts lack high-speed connectivity, creating unequal justice tiers.
      • Erosion of Judicial Conscience: Syllogistic code cannot express moral equity or human mercy.
